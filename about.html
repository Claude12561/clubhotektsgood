OUR IDENTITY

Vision, Mission & Background

Learn about the foundation that drives CLUB HOTEK TSS forward.

Our Vision

To become a leading TVET institution that produces competent, innovative and professional graduates who contribute to the socio-economic development of Rwanda and beyond.

Our Mission

To provide quality, practical and market-oriented training that equips learners with the skills, knowledge and attitudes needed for employment, self-employment and lifelong learning.

Our Background

CLUB HOTEK TSS was established to address the growing demand for practical skills among young people in Rwanda. Located in Gisozi Sector, Gasabo District, the school offers a variety of TVET programs designed to prepare learners for real-world careers and entrepreneurial opportunities.

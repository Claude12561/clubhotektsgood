You can register by visiting the school in person at Gisozi Sector, Gasabo District, or by calling 0788301833 / 0788627310. You may also use the online application form on the Admissions section of this website.

CLUB HOTEK TSS offers six main TVET programs: Food & Beverage Operations, Automobile Mechanics, Culinary Arts, Tailoring, Hairdressing and Multimedia.

Requirements vary by program. Generally, applicants should have completed at least primary education (P6) or Ordinary Level (S3), depending on the trade. Please contact the school for detailed and updated requirements for your chosen program.

You can reach us by phone at 0788301833 or 0788627310, by email at clubhotek@gmail.com, or via WhatsApp at +250 788 301 833.

CLUB HOTEK TSS is located in Gisozi Sector, Gasabo District, Kigali, Rwanda.
